Hello World

This is my first ever blog post! As an introduction. I am Pete, a PhD student in public health simulation in the University of Sheffield. I am funded by the Wellcome Trust. My research is on how should we model social interaction in agent-based models of public health. Up until now, most model are grounded in social theory ,with little to no information about real-world social structures directly taken from the system being modelled.
